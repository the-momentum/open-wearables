## Interpreting the Scores

Both scores use a 0-100 scale. A higher score indicates a better outcome.

| Score            | Scale  | Higher score means        |
|------------------|--------|---------------------------|
| Sleep Score      | 0-100  | Better sleep quality      |
| Resilience Score | 0-100  | Better HRV consistency    |

Neither score is a diagnostic tool. They are intended to surface trends and patterns over time.

---

## Provider Scores vs. OW Native Scores

In addition to the OW native scores described above, users may also see scores fetched directly from their connected device providers - for example, a sleep score from Garmin or a readiness score from Oura. These provider scores are always clearly labelled with their source and are kept separate from the OW computed scores. They do not influence each other.

This means you can surface both in your application - the OW scores for a consistent cross-device baseline, and the provider scores for users who want to compare against what their device reports.

---

## Disabling OW Native Scores

Self-hosted deployments compute both OW scores by default. To turn them off, set `OW_SCORES_ENABLED=false` in the backend `.env`.

Provider scores are still stored and shown. Scores computed before the switch stay in the database. Re-enabling backfills the last `SCORE_BACKFILL_DAYS` (30 by default).

---

## Deprecation of OW native scores

On November 6, 2026 the Sleep Score and Resilience Score computed by Open Wearables are removed from this repository. They will be distributed as a separate package, available as an extension under a different license.

What stays the same:

- The [`GET /api/v1/users/{user_id}/health-scores`](/api-reference/external:-health-scores/list-health-scores) endpoint, its parameters and response format.
- Scores fetched from connected providers (Garmin, Oura, Whoop, Polar, and others).
- OW scores computed before November 6. They stay in the database and the endpoint keeps returning them.

What changes: Open Wearables no longer computes new Sleep Score and Resilience Score records.

To check whether your integration uses OW native scores, look for records with `"provider": "internal"` in the response. You can also filter for them directly:

```bash
GET /api/v1/users/{user_id}/health-scores?provider=internal
```

These are the records that are no longer created after November 6:

| Score            | `provider` | `category`   |
|------------------|------------|--------------|
| Sleep Score      | `internal` | `sleep`      |
| Resilience Score | `internal` | `resilience` |

Sleep scores from providers use the same `sleep` category but carry the provider name (for example `"provider": "oura"`), so they are not affected.
